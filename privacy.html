Privacy Policy – Topple Pix

Developer: BatVeil (Eraycan Merih) · Contact: batveilgames@gmail.com · Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Topple Pix ("the game") handles, why, and the choices you have. BatVeil does not run any servers for the game and does not receive your personal information.

1. Information stored only on your device

  • Game progress, scores, coins, settings and the items you unlocked.
  • An age group (under 13, 13–17, or 18+) chosen on the first-launch age screen. The birth year you tap is not stored.
  • Whether you bought "No Ads", and receipts of coin purchases already delivered, so a purchase is never delivered twice.

This information never leaves your phone through us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: optional reward videos (for example to revive a run) and occasional full-screen ads between games. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google handles this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Players under 13: every ad request is marked as child-directed, limited to general-audience (G) content and non-personalised.
  • Players aged 13–17: ad requests are marked as under the age of consent and are non-personalised.
  • Players 18+ in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent form asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown. You can change it at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases ("No Ads" and coin packs) are processed entirely by Google Play. BatVeil never sees your payment details. Google Play tells the game which items your account owns so they can be delivered and restored. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Reminder notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules reminders on your phone (for example when your free chest is ready or a new Daily Run starts). They are created and stored only on your device; no server and no personal data are involved. Turn them off in the game under Settings → Reminders, or in your Android notification settings.

5. Analytics

The game does not use any analytics service and does not send gameplay data anywhere.

6. Children

Topple Pix is designed for players aged 13 and over. Younger players who reach it receive child-directed, non-personalised ads only, and the game asks no one for a name, email, photo, contacts or location. Parents can contact us at the address above with any question.

7. Your rights

Because BatVeil holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Data collected by Google for ads or purchases can be managed at myaccount.google.com. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you can change your ad consent in the game at any time and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
